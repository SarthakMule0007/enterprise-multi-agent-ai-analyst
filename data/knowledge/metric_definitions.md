Revenue means the sum of the order revenue field in the selected period. It is recorded in USD and is not adjusted for refunds or discounts beyond the recorded order value.

Order count is the count of distinct order_id values. Each row in the orders table represents one order.

Average order revenue is total revenue divided by the number of orders in the same group. It is not an average per item.

Regions are the sales territories recorded on each order: North, South, East, and West.

Product category is the category assigned at order time. Category values in this sample are Electronics, Home, Office, and Outdoors.
